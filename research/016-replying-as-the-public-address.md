## 1. Resend free tier, SMTP, quota, acceptable use

- **SMTP relay is listed on the Free plan.** The pricing table has an "SMTP relay" row with the same check icon in all four columns (Free, Pro, Scale, Enterprise); the row's tooltip reads "...emails at scale through a reliable SMTP server." https://resend.com/pricing (2026-11-15). Icon only, no text saying "included on Free": treat as likely yes, UNVERIFIED until a test connection succeeds.
- **SMTP settings.** Host `smtp.resend.com`; ports 25, 465, 587, 2465, 2587; username `resend`; password is the API key; 465/2465 implicit TLS, 25/587/2587 STARTTLS. Needs "A verified domain". https://resend.com/docs/send-with-smtp (2026-11-15).
- **Same quota and rate limit.** "Does the rate limit apply when sending with SMTP? Yes, the rate limit is the same as the API." Sent SMTP mail shows in the emails table. https://resend.com/docs/send-with-smtp (2026-11-15). Quota: "Transactional emails: daily email quota of 100 emails/day and 3,000 emails/month. This quota includes both sent and received emails. Multiple To, CC, or BCC recipients ... count as separate emails." https://resend.com/docs/knowledge-base/resend-sending-limits (2026-11-15). The page does not say SMTP sends have a separate quota; same-quota is therefore inference from "same rate limit" (UNVERIFIED for quota). Replies and form messages share the 100/day, so a reply spike also eats the form's headroom (ticket 015 accepted the cap, with fallback to the Public address).
- **No SMTP server logs.** "We currently don't provide SMTP server logs for debugging." https://resend.com/docs/send-with-smtp (2026-11-15). Matters for diagnosing a failed phone setup.
- **Acceptable use vs personal replies.** The AUP prohibits "unsolicited messages of any kind, including cold outreach, purchased lists, or scraped contact data" and says "All mail must be sent to recipients who have explicitly opted in to receive communications from you." https://resend.com/legal/acceptable-use (2026-11-15). It does not mention one-to-one or personal correspondence. A reply to someone who wrote to the owner first is not unsolicited on any plain reading, but no line says personal replies are in scope or out of it: UNVERIFIED (my inference). Bounce limit stated as lower than 4%, complaints also capped, "your account may be shut down without warning" above thresholds (same page); a handful of replies a week is far below any volume signal but a bad address would count.
- **Terms.** Free Tier is "subject to usage limits and feature restrictions as described on our pricing page"; Resend may "modify the features, limits, or availability of the Free Tier" or "cancel or discontinue" it at any time without notice. https://resend.com/legal/terms-of-service section 6 (2026-11-15). So SMTP-on-free could change.
- **From address.** SMTP needs the sending domain verified. Ticket 015 verifies the root with SPF+MX on `send.` and DKIM on `resend._domainkey`, so `name@jpsantos.eu` as From should pass SPF/DKIM alignment (inference from 014/015, not re-tested here).

## 2. Mail apps: read Gmail over IMAP, send as the Public address via Resend SMTP

Gmail's position: "Your experience remains unchanged if you use a third-party email client. For example, Outlook, Thunderbird, or Apple Mail can still access your Gmail account through IMAP or POP." and "Access through the Gmail mobile app ... will not change." https://support.google.com/mail/answer/17101213 (2026-11-15). Send-as for third-party addresses is gone in January 2027 "in Gmail on the web or in the Gmail mobile apps". Same page lists as recommended paths "a dedicated mail app, a desktop mail client, or the Gmail mobile app".

Gmail IMAP in a third-party app needs an app password or OAuth; ImprovMX's guides require 2-Step Verification plus a Gmail App Password for Apple Mail. https://improvmx.com/guides/apple-mail-ios-smtp (2026-11-15), vendor guide, UNVERIFIED against a Google page.

**Does Gmail's own Sent folder get a copy?** Gmail's own copy comes from Gmail's SMTP server; mail sent through Resend does not pass through Gmail. Whether a copy appears depends on the client appending to the IMAP Sent folder. Thunderbird has "Place a copy in" under Copies & Folders ("make a copy of messages that you send, and place them in the folder you choose") https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/configuration-options-accounts (search summary, 2026-11-15); forum advice is to untick it for providers that save sent mail themselves, so for Resend-via-Thunderbird it should stay on and point to Gmail's `[Gmail]/Sent Mail`. Apple Mail and Android: not established. Overall UNVERIFIED; test once per client. Resend itself keeps a log of sent mail (the emails table, https://resend.com/docs/send-with-smtp) as a fallback record.

**Threading and Reply-To.** The form sets the visitor as Reply-To (ticket 010/015), so a mail-app Reply goes to the visitor, From the Public address (set per identity/account). The visitor's next reply goes to the Public address, via ImprovMX into Gmail, where Gmail threads by headers and subject (inference, UNVERIFIED; not tested). Resend documents a threading control for SMTP: "Prevent threading on Gmail with the `X-Entity-Ref-ID` header" https://resend.com/docs/send-with-smtp (2026-11-15), which implies Gmail can thread Resend mail by subject and so replies may merge into the original thread. Loop risk: ImprovMX warns "Avoid replying to your alias from the same Gmail you're forwarding to - this creates a mail loop" (about Gmail send-as, https://improvmx.com/guides/send-emails-using-gmail, vendor); not applicable when the visitor, not the Public address, is the recipient.

## 3. Zoho Mail Forever Free

- **Plan and limits.** "Forever Free Plan *: Email hosting for one domain for up to 5 users, 5 GB Mail storage per user, IMAP/POP/Active Sync not included. *Available only in select data centers." https://www.zoho.eu/mail/zohomail-pricing.html (2026-11-15). Zoho's compare page: "up to five users on one custom domain with 5 GB each, only in the US, EU, and India Data Centers, without IMAP/POP/ActiveSync"; "free-plan users are limited to webmail and the mobile apps." https://www.zoho.com/mail/compare/overview.html (2026-11-15). Zoho subscription help: "host one domain for free with web-only access." https://www.zoho.com/mail/help/adminconsole/subscription.html (2026-11-15).
- **New signup now.** The pricing page offers "Sign Up Now" for the free plan (2026-11-15), so it exists. Whether a given signup gets it depends on region: "If free plans are not available in your region, you will be required to upgrade to a paid plan ... mail operations for all users will be disabled" after the 15-day trial (subscription help, same URL). That the data centre is chosen by IP location at signup came from a search summary only: UNVERIFIED. The EU data centre is listed as supported for free; the owner would be on `zoho.eu`.
- **IMAP/SMTP.** None on free. A Zoho community post by a long-time free user confirms the change: IMAP and POP show "Available exclusively with paid plans" (https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/community/topic/no-more-imap-pop-smtp-on-free-plans-even-on-referrals-with-no-notice, user post, undated in extract). So Thunderbird/Apple Mail cannot read or send via it. Sending is webmail or Zoho's iOS/Android apps only.
- **Forward to Gmail.** Not available to new free signups: "For the new users who signed up under the Free plan, the Email Forwarding feature will not be available." https://www.zoho.com/mail/help/email-forwarding.html (2026-11-15). Help Scout's Zoho guide says the same of Forever Free (https://docs.helpscout.com/article/1753-auto-forward-from-zoho-mail, third-party, undated). So Zoho would replace Gmail as the place the owner reads and writes the Public address, not feed it.
- **What replacing ImprovMX changes (DNS).** MX on the root becomes Zoho's (`mx.zoho.*` priority 10, plus the lower-priority pair; TLD matches the data centre, e.g. `.eu`) and "Having MX records from multiple providers ... can cause delivery conflicts." https://www.zoho.com/mail/help/adminconsole/configure-email-delivery.html (2026-11-15). SPF: Zoho's published record is `v=spf1 include:zohomail.com -all` (https://www.zoho.com/mail/help/adminconsole/spf-configuration.html, 2026-11-15; the EU include value for `zoho.eu` is not confirmed here, UNVERIFIED: the Admin Console shows the correct one), which must be merged with Resend's SPF, which sits on `send.` and so does not collide with the root. DKIM: Zoho issues its own selector and Resend's stays on `resend._domainkey`. ImprovMX MX and SPF come out of the plan in ticket 015's DNS list. Ticket 010's "delivered to an ImprovMX alias" would become "delivered to a Zoho mailbox/alias"; the form's To address, Reply-To visitor and fallback address are unchanged. DMARC reports still go to the Public address (now readable in Zoho only).
- **Replying.** In Zoho webmail/app the From is the mailbox address on `jpsantos.eu`; sent mail is stored in Zoho's Sent. Not researched beyond that. The cost: the owner reads contact mail in a second inbox (Zoho app) instead of Gmail, and Zoho's inactive-account policy applies: a user not logged in for "more than 120 days" is flagged as inactive (https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/community/topic/details-limitations-of-the-free-forever-plan sidebar notice, Zoho staff, 2026-11-15; wording from an announcement excerpt).
- **Gmail-pull alternative.** Not possible: Gmail no longer fetches third-party accounts with POP on the web after January 2027 and Zoho free has no POP anyway.

## 5. Replying from Gmail as-is

The visitor sees the owner's Gmail address as sender; if the reply is sent via Gmail's send-as with an alias, Outlook-style clients "might see something like 'From yourname@gmail.com on behalf of othername@otherdomain.com'" (https://support.google.com/mail/answer/22370, 2026-11-15), but send-as for third-party addresses is gone after January 2027. Replying plainly from Gmail exposes the Gmail address (the very thing the Public address exists to hide). The visitor can also reply to the Gmail address directly afterwards.
